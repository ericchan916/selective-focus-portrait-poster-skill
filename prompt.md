## Full prompt (copy everything below)

```
Series Name: {{FOCAL IDENTITY}}
Main Title: {{MAIN TITLE}}
Subject: {{age, gender, identity, profession}}
Name: {{NAME}}
Role: {{ROLE}}
Key Line: {{KEY LINE}}
Accent Color: {{yellow / red / cobalt blue / rust orange}}
Focus Method: {{horizontal sharp slice / floating focus window / vertical sharp slice / offset focus frame}}
Aspect Ratio: {{9:16}}

If any field is empty, fill it yourself so that subject, role, key line and title express one concept; choose the focus method and accent color that best fit it; use a fictional name. Do not depict or name any real, identifiable person unless I supplied them. Do not ask questions.

Create a sophisticated editorial portrait poster built around selective focus.

Portrait: a realistic photographic portrait with natural skin texture, pores, subtle imperfections, believable hair and authentic facial structure. Avoid plastic skin, CGI smoothness, heavy beauty retouching and generic commercial-model styling.

Selective focus: most of the portrait is softly blurred, slightly motion-softened, desaturated or reduced in detail. Only the area defined by the Focus Method returns to sharp detail, natural skin tone and clear texture. Sharp and blurred areas must align perfectly as the same person in the same moment — no duplicated eyes, no second face, no pasted-photo effect.

Focus methods:
- horizontal sharp slice: a sharp horizontal band across the eyes
- floating focus window: a small floating window isolating part of the face
- vertical sharp slice: a narrow vertical sharp slice cutting through motion blur
- offset focus frame: a larger offset frame shifting attention away from the facial center

Color — Neutral Field + One Accent Color: a restrained neutral palette of charcoal, gray, warm beige, off-white and natural skin tones. The Accent Color is the only strong accent, used for the focus lines, the main title and a few key details.

Typography: a high-contrast editorial serif for the Main Title; a condensed bold sans-serif for Name and Role; a small neutral sans-serif for secondary information; the Key Line as meaningful copy tied to the subject and concept.

Composition: asymmetric, with generous negative space; portrait, typography and focus geometry interact.

Finish: subtle film grain, light scan texture, restrained print texture — one finished editorial piece that feels like a polished independent fashion, photography, music or culture magazine poster, where blur and sharpness control the eye.

Avoid: cyberpunk HUDs, excessive UI graphics, neon gradients, gibberish text, logos, watermarks, numbering, sparkles, harsh Gaussian blur, melted features, duplicated features, plastic skin, over-retouching.

Output one finished poster only. Do not show analysis or alternatives.
```
